Privacy notice

Last updated: [date]

Movepadi is a community guide to public transport in Nigeria, built from trips that commuters share. This notice explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We have written it in plain language, because we want you to understand it.

The short version: you can use Movepadi without giving your name, email or phone number. We ask for payout details only if you choose to cash out rewards. We do not sell your personal data.

Who we are

Movepadi is run by [your business name and registered address] (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at [your contact email].

What we collect

  • Trips you share. Places, vehicles, landmarks, fares, waiting and walking times, how safe it felt, and any tips you write. Please do not include names, phone numbers, vehicle plate numbers or your home address. Use an area or junction instead.
  • An anonymous account. When you visit, we create a random account ID so the app can remember your trips, votes and rewards. It does not contain your name. It is kept in your browser, and if you clear your browser data you can lose it, unless you save it with an email address.
  • Your email (optional). Only if you choose “Save my account”. We use it to send you a sign-in link.
  • Payout details (only if you cash out). A phone number for airtime or data, or a bank name and account number for a transfer. These are visible only to you and to Movepadi administrators, and are used only to pay you.
  • Votes and rewards. Which trips you confirmed, and a record of what you earned and were paid.
  • Technical information. Our security check (Cloudflare Turnstile) and our hosting providers process things like your IP address and browser type to keep the service safe and working.

How we use it

  • To run Movepadi: show trips, let other commuters confirm them, and build route guides.
  • To calculate and pay rewards, and to check for fraud, duplicate accounts and abuse.
  • To keep the service secure, fix problems and understand how it is used.

Who can see what

  • Other users see trips without any information about who shared them.
  • Movepadi team members see trips, short account references (not names), and, for administrators, payout requests and balances.
  • Service providers that help us run Movepadi: Supabase (database and sign-in), Vercel (website hosting), Cloudflare (security check), and any airtime, data or bank payment provider we use to pay you. They process data on our behalf.
  • The public may see counts of confirmed trips and the outline of confirmed routes (places, fare and time) on our progress page, never who shared them.

We do not sell your personal data. Information about routes, fares and travel times may be published as route guides or shared in a combined form that does not identify you.

Where your data is stored

Our providers may store and process data on servers outside Nigeria. We choose providers with appropriate security practices and take steps to protect your data when it is transferred, as required by Nigerian data protection law.

How long we keep it

  • Trips are kept while Movepadi operates, and may be kept in a form that no longer links to your account.
  • Payout details and payment records are kept as long as needed to make payments and meet accounting and legal duties ([state period]).
  • If you ask us to delete your account, we will delete or anonymise your data unless we must keep some of it by law.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you can ask us for access to your data, correction, deletion, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to withdraw consent where we rely on it. Because accounts are anonymous, we may need you to show us you control the account (for example, the email you saved) before we act. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Contact us at [your contact email].

Security

We use access controls, encryption in transit and restrictions on who can see payout details. No system is perfectly secure, so please avoid putting sensitive personal information in a trip.

Children

Movepadi is not for people under 18, and you must be 18 or older to cash out rewards.

Changes

If we change this notice in a way that matters, we will update the date above and tell you in the app.
