1. What Movepadi is

Movepadi is a community guide to public transport in Nigeria. Everything on it comes from trips that commuters share and confirm. It is early-stage, and it can change or have mistakes.

2. Using Movepadi

  • You can use Movepadi without giving your name. You must be 18 or older to cash out rewards.

  • Keep your account details and payout details safe.

3. The trips you share

  • Only share a trip you actually made, and describe it honestly.

4. Confirming other people’s trips
